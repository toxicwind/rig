# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in Rig a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

**Welcome:** using welcoming language, respecting viewpoints, gracefully accepting
criticism, focusing on what's best for the community, showing empathy.

**Not welcome:** sexualized language or imagery, trolling, insults, personal or
political attacks, harassment (public or private), publishing others' private
information, or any conduct reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the maintainers at
[https://github.com/toxicwind/rig/issues](https://github.com/toxicwind/rig/issues).
Maintainers will review and respond, and may remove, edit, or reject contributions
that don't align with this Code of Conduct.
